# production-engineering-dairies

I'm a backend engineer with 7+ years of experience building and operating distributed systems, with a focus on payments, cloud-native architectures, and reliable backend services.

Over the years, I've worked on systems where correctness, scalability, and availability are essential. My experience spans Java, Kotlin, AWS, microservices, and the operational challenges that come with running production systems at scale.

Production Engineering Diaries is where I document the lessons I've learned along the way—the incidents that challenged my assumptions, the debugging journeys that revealed hidden problems, and the engineering decisions that helped make systems more resilient.This repository is a collection of stories from production: incidents I investigated, Kubernetes edge cases I encountered, distributed systems failures I debugged, and the lessons I took away from each experience.

Each article is written from my perspective, walking through what I observed, the assumptions I made, what turned out to be wrong, how we identified the root cause, and the changes that followed. My goal isn't just to explain how technologies like Kubernetes or microservices work, but to share what it's actually like to operate production systems, make decisions under pressure, and continuously learn from real-world failures.

If you're interested in production engineering, Kubernetes, distributed systems, platform engineering, or just enjoy reading real engineering stories, I hope you find something useful here.
